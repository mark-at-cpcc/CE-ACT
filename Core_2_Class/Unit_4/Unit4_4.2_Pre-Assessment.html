No Preparation Needed

Take this before you begin the Unit 4 reading and videos, and do not study for it first. It measures what you bring with you to Unit 4. Security has more everyday vocabulary than any unit so far — you have probably heard of phishing and ransomware — so expect a mix of terms you half-know and terms you have never seen. This page stays available all unit, and you can retake it as often as you like.

Read This First

You are expected to get questions wrong. You have not been taught this material yet. The purpose is to prime your brain: research on the testing effect shows that attempting to retrieve an answer you do not have makes the correct answer stick better when you meet it later. Guessing today is doing real work.
