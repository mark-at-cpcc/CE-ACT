Active Directory Is 27 Minutes — Give It Its Own Sitting

This is the longest single video in the entire Core 2 course, and it is dense. Watch it when you are fresh, not at the end of an evening, and keep the Part 1 section of Section 5.3 open beside it. Domains, organizational units, and security groups are the machinery behind everything you learned about least privilege in Unit 4.

Watch Order

The table is in the order I recommend, which follows our class sessions. Watch the four 2.2 videos after Tuesday's class, while the workstation hardening is fresh — splitting Active Directory into its own evening. Watch the 2.3, 2.8, 2.10, and 2.11 videos before Wednesday. Watch Change Management and Managing Backups before Thursday.

How to Watch These

  • Name the Unit 4 threat. Nearly every control in this unit answers an attack you met last unit. When he introduces a setting, pause and say what it stops. That pairing is what scenario questions are built on.
  • Pause and do — where you can. The 2.2 and 2.11 videos cover settings on your own Windows machine and your own browser. Open them and follow along. The router and Active Directory material is watch-only until NETLAB.
  • One sheet per objective. Seven sheets this unit. This mirrors how the exam is organized.
  • Write the summary, not the transcript. After each video, close your notes and write two sentences on what it was about.

Video and Book Are Not Redundant

This unit splits cleanly. The videos are the better way to see a setting configured — where it lives, what the screen looks like, which box to tick. The book is the better way to understand the trade-off behind it, which is what a scenario question tests. Messer will show you where to turn on a pop-up blocker; the chapter explains why private browsing does not make a user anonymous.

What Good Notes Look Like

Video notes are not graded. Organize them by objective number, one sheet per objective, in your own words. For 2.2, 2.10, and 2.11, every entry should say where the setting lives and what it prevents. See the Course Schedule for the target date.
