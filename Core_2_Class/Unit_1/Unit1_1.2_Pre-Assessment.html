No Preparation Needed

Do not study for this and do not attempt it in advance. It measures what you bring with you on day one of Core 2. You use an operating system every day, so some of these will feel familiar — others will not, and that is the point. This page stays available all week, and you can retake it as many times as you like once the material starts making sense.

Read This First

You are expected to get questions wrong. You have not been taught this material yet. The purpose is to prime your brain: research on the testing effect shows that attempting to retrieve an answer you do not have makes the correct answer stick better when you meet it later. Guessing today is doing real work.

Select an answer for each question, then click Grade My Answers at the bottom to see your results. Nothing here is locked in until you click that button, and you can click Reset afterward to clear everything and try again. The answer choices appear in a new order each time.
