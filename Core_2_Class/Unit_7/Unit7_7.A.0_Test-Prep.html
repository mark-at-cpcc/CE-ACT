Unit 7 is the bridge between the last day of this course and the day you sit the real exam. It opens alongside Unit 6, and it stays useful long after the course ends.

Where Unit 7 Fits

Unit 7 has no class meetings of its own. It opens at the same time as Unit 6, and the Final Study Exam is taken in class on Unit 6's last day. Everything else here is yours to use at your own pace — during the final week and in the days between the end of the course and your exam date. Every date is in the Course Schedule.

What Unit 7 Helps You Do

  1. Find the gaps in what you know, objective by objective, before you spend another hour studying
  2. Review all four exam domains quickly, with the facts most likely to be tested in front of you
  3. Measure your readiness with a full-length, timed practice exam scored the way the real one is
  4. Prepare for exam day itself: the rules, the format, the strategy, and booking
  5. Decide, with evidence, when you are ready to book the exam
In Plain Terms

Units 1 through 6 taught you the material. Unit 7 helps you prove it. It tells you what you still do not know, gives you a short way to review everything, tests you under real conditions, and walks you through exam day so nothing about it surprises you.

The Order That Works

  1. Read the brain-dump section first. It opens Section 7.A.1 and takes five minutes. It protects your certification before you go looking for study material.
  2. Do the Six-Week Audit. Mark every objective confident, shaky, or unknown. The result is your "still shaky" list, and it decides where the rest of your time goes.
  3. Review with the Study Guide. Work through Section 7.A.2 with your shaky list beside it. For anything that is still not clear, go back to that unit's content review.
  4. Take the Final Study Exam, timed and closed-book. Score it by domain, not just overall.
  5. Study your misses, then take a second timed attempt. The gap between attempts is what makes the second one meaningful.
  6. Make the readiness decision. Two consecutive timed attempts at 780 or better, with no weak domain. Then book, using the steps in Section 7.A.1.
  7. Use the final-week checklist in Section 7.A.1 from two weeks out until exam day.

Why This Order

Studying everything again feels productive and changes very little. Finding your gaps first, reviewing only those, and then testing yourself under real conditions is the fastest route from "I took the course" to "I am ready to pass." Each step in Unit 7 feeds the next one.

Nothing in Unit 7 Is Graded

Unit 7 has no graded work. Its only measure is the one that matters: whether you are ready to pass the real exam. Treat the Final Study Exam as seriously as the real one, because its score is the best evidence you will have when you decide to book.

Unit 7 Checklist

  • Read the brain-dump section at the start of Section 7.A.1
  • Complete the Six-Week Audit in Section 7.A.1 and keep your "still shaky" list
  • Review the Section 7.A.2 Study Guide against that list
  • Take the Section 7.A.3 Final Study Exam, timed and closed-book, in class on Unit 6's last day
  • Retake it after studying your misses, until you have two consecutive attempts at 780 or better
  • Book your exam, then follow the final-week checklist in Section 7.A.1
