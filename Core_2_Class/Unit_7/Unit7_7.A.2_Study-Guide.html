1.0 Operating Systems — 28%

TopicKnow This Cold
File systemsNTFS: permissions and encryption. FAT32: 4 GB file limit. exFAT: large files, PC and Mac. ext4/XFS: Linux. APFS: macOS. ReFS: resilient server storage.
EditionsHome has no domain join, BitLocker, Group Policy Editor, or Remote Desktop host. Pro and Enterprise do. Windows 11 needs TPM 2.0, UEFI with Secure Boot, 4 GB RAM, 64 GB storage.
InstallsClean vs. in-place upgrade vs. image deployment vs. repair. GPT (UEFI, modern) vs. MBR (legacy). Check requirements and back up first.
Tools (.msc)eventvwr (logs) · diskmgmt (volumes) · taskschd (schedules) · devmgmt (drivers, roll back) · certmgr (user certificates) · lusrmgr (local accounts) · perfmon (counters over time) · gpedit (policy). Also: Task Manager, msconfig, msinfo32, resmon, regedit.
Command lineipconfig · ping · tracert · pathping · nslookup · netstat · net use / net user · gpupdate (apply) vs. gpresult (report) · sfc · chkdsk (/f fix, /r bad sectors) · diskpart · format · robocopy (/mir deletes) · whoami · hostname. Many need an administrator prompt.
Settings and networkingShow file extensions. Sleep vs. hibernate. USB selective suspend. Workgroup vs. domain. 169.254.x.x = no DHCP answer. Public vs. private profile. Proxy, metered connections, VPN.
macOS and LinuxmacOS: Force Quit, Time Machine, FileVault, Keychain, Spotlight, Gatekeeper, .dmg/.pkg. Linux: ls, cd, cp, mv, rm, chmod, chown, sudo, df, du, grep, ps, top, apt/dnf, /etc/fstab.

2.0 Security — 28%

TopicKnow This Cold
ControlsPhysical: bollards, access control vestibule (stops tailgating), badges, cameras, locks. Logical: least privilege, Zero Trust, MFA, SSO, ACLs, MDM, DLP.
Malware by symptomRansomware: renamed files, payment note. Cryptominer: CPU pegged, fan running. Keylogger: stolen logins. Rootkit: hides from scans. Trojan: posed as useful software. Adware/PUP: pop-ups. Fileless: lives in memory.
Social engineering and threatsPhishing (email), vishing (voice), smishing (text), QR phishing, spear phishing (targeted), whaling (executives), tailgating, shoulder surfing, impersonation, dumpster diving. Business email compromise, evil twin, on-path, zero-day, DoS/DDoS, brute force vs. dictionary, SQL injection, XSS, insider, supply chain.
Windows securityOver the network, the more restrictive of share and NTFS permissions wins. UAC makes elevation a decision. BitLocker (volume), BitLocker To Go (USB), EFS (files). Disable Guest; set account lockout.
Wireless and SOHOWPA3, or WPA2 with AES; TKIP is deprecated. RADIUS/TACACS+ for individual sign-in; Kerberos for domains. Router: change default password first, update firmware, disable UPnP, guest network, restrict management.
Mobile and browserEncryption plus screen lock; locator, remote wipe, remote backup, MDM/BYOD. Trusted sources and hashes, extension permissions, certificate warnings, private browsing is not anonymity.
DisposalFormatting is not destruction. Wipe to reuse; drill, shred, or incinerate to destroy. Degaussing does not work on SSDs. Get a certificate of destruction.

Malware Removal, in Order

1 Investigate and verify → 2 Quarantine → 3 Disable System Restore → 4 Remediate → 5 Update anti-malware → 6 Scan in safe mode or a preinstallation environment → 7 Reimage if needed → 8 Schedule scans and updates → 9 Enable System Restore and create a restore point → 10 Educate the user.

Method: identify the problem → theory of probable cause → test the theory → plan and implement → verify and prevent → document. When several fixes work, choose the least disruptive one: restart a service before rebooting, repair before reimaging.

4.0 Operational Procedures — 21%

TopicKnow This Cold
DocumentationTickets: user, device, description, category, severity, escalation, progress notes, resolution. Knowledge base, SOPs, onboarding/offboarding checklists, SLAs, asset tags and inventory.
Change managementStandard (pre-approved), normal (board approval), emergency (documented afterward). Rollback plan, sandbox test, change freeze vs. maintenance window, peer review, end-user acceptance.
Backup and recoveryFull; incremental (since last backup — slow restore); differential (since last full — two-piece restore); synthetic full. GFS rotation. 3-2-1: three copies, two media, one offsite. Test restores.
Safety and environmentESD strap, mat, antistatic bags; disconnect power; lift with legs; goggles and masks. MSDS for disposal. Surge suppressor for spikes; UPS for power loss.
Policy and privacyIncident response: stop, preserve, escalate; chain of custody; order of volatility. Licenses: EULA, perpetual, personal vs. corporate, open source. Regulated data: PII, healthcare, payment cards. AUP, splash screens.
Scripting.bat, .ps1, .vbs, .sh, .js, .py. Automates restarts, drive mapping, installs, backups. Risks: malware, unintended setting changes, resource crashes. Read before you run.
Remote access and AIRDP, VPN, VNC, SSH, RMM. Never expose RDP to the internet: VPN plus MFA. AI drafts, people decide; verify before acting; keep sensitive data out; never use AI during an exam.
ProfessionalismSet expectations, avoid jargon, listen without interrupting, respect privacy, follow up.

Before You Sit It

Two timed practice exams at 780 or better. Never use brain dumps. On exam day, flag performance-based questions and return to them, check the time at 30 and 60 minutes, and answer every question — there is no penalty for guessing. Full details are in Section 7.A.1.
