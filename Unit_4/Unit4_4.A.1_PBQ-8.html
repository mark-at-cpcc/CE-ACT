4.A.1 — PBQ 8

Identify Wi-Fi Band Support

A performance-based question on 802.11 wireless standards, the Wi-Fi generation each belongs to, and the frequency bands each one supports.

How This Works

Five Wi-Fi generations (Wi-Fi 1–7) are shown in the left column, drawn at random from the eight covered in this unit's Content Review. For each one, use the Standard dropdown to select the matching 802.11 standard, then use the remaining dropdowns to select Yes or No for whether that standard supports the 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz bands. Click Score to check your answers, and Reset to draw a fresh set of five Wi-Fi generations.

For each Wi-Fi generation, select its 802.11 standard and Yes or No for each frequency band it supports.
