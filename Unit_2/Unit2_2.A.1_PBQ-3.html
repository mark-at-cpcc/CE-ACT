Match Each Letter to Its Connector

A performance-based question, matching the drag-and-drop format used on the real CompTIA exam.

How This Works

Seven connector photos are shown in the center column, each labeled A through G. Drag the correct name for each connector from the pool on the right onto its matching lettered slot on the left. Some tiles are decoys — they name a real connector, just not one of the seven pictured. Click Score to check your answers, and Reset to shuffle and try again.

Cable or Port?

Three of these connector types appear twice below — once as the cable end, once as the port it plugs into. Read each photo closely; a cable and its matching port share a name, but that name still needs the right ending on the tile.
