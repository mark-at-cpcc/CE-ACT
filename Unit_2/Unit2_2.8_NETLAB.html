Three labs this unit, roughly 45 minutes to 1.5 hours each.

What NETLAB Actually Is

NETLAB+ gives you temporary access to a real virtual machine or lab environment through your browser — no installation, and nothing on your own computer is at risk. You book a slot, do the lab, and when your time is up the system wipes the machine clean for the next student. You cannot permanently break anything, which is the whole point.

Technical Overview

NETLAB+ provisions pods of physical and virtual equipment to reservation-based student sessions. Each pod is allocated exclusively for the duration of the reservation and reset to a clean baseline on release. Because pods are finite and allocated per course, availability is genuinely constrained near deadlines.

Getting In

  1. Always launch from Brightspace. Each lab has its own link inside this unit. Do not navigate to the NETLAB site directly — launching from the course shell is what associates your session with this class and your roster entry.
  2. Make a reservation. Choose a date and time window. NETLAB allocates a pod to you for exactly that period.
  3. Read the worksheet before your window opens. Worksheets live in the NETLAB — Lab Worksheets folder under Coursework in Brightspace, and inside NETLAB itself. Reading during your reservation burns time you paid for.
  4. Complete the lab in one continuous session, capturing screenshots as you go.
  5. Submit screenshots to the matching lab quiz in Brightspace.
One Session, No Save Point

If your reservation expires mid-lab, the environment resets and you begin again from step one. There is no pause, no saved progress, and no partial completion.

Book generously. If a lab is estimated at 1 hour, reserve 1.5. Finishing early costs you nothing; running out costs you the entire attempt.

Pods Are Limited — Reserve Early

This course has a finite number of lab pods. When the whole class books the same Sunday night, the slots are simply gone, and the deadline does not move because a reservation was unavailable.

Book your window early in the week, not the night before it's due.

Screenshots go directly into the lab quiz's question field — not attached as separate files. Each screenshot must clearly show the NETLAB timestamp and your reservation ID. Screenshots without both are not accepted as proof of completion.

Before You Submit
