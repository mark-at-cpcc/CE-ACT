How to Watch These

Passive watching produces the same warm familiarity as passive reading, and just as little retention. Three habits change that:

  • Pause and predict. When Messer poses a question or introduces a comparison, pause and answer before he does. Getting it wrong is more useful than hearing it right.
  • One sheet per objective. Not per video — per objective number. Both 1.2 videos and both 1.3 videos land on one page each, so you can compare related concepts side by side.
  • Watch Virtualization Services and Cloud Models twice. Both domains are brand new this week and dense with vocabulary. A second pass after you've read 5.3 and 5.4 will land differently than the first.
