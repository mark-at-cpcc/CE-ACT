Technical Overview

This practice set is drawn at random and weighted to the published 220-1201 domain percentages, so each set you work reflects the same mix you will face on the live exam. Options are shuffled on every draw. You will get a per-domain breakdown when you submit.

In Plain Terms

Work this set more than once. The point is not to reach a score and stop — it is to keep drawing new sets until the same domain stops showing up in your misses. Work each set closed book and in one sitting, the way you will have to on exam day. If you find yourself recognizing an item rather than reasoning through it, that is your cue to go back to 6.3 and 6.4 rather than draw again.

Track Your Guesses

Keep a sheet next to you and mark every item you were not sure about, even the ones you got right. Confident and wrong is the category that fails people at the testing center, and it is completely invisible on a score report. An item you narrowed to two and guessed correctly belongs on your review list next to the ones you missed outright.

Cumulative Practice Set

Answer every item, then submit for your domain breakdown.

Reading Your Breakdown

A domain that comes back weak on one set might be noise. The same domain coming back weak on three sets is a gap, and that is the signal to stop practicing and go read. Practice questions reveal gaps; they do not close them. The closing happens in 6.3, in the chapter review material from 6.4, and in the Study Group sessions you picked in 6.5.

Submission

Submit through the Brightspace link for this section by the due date. Full credit requires completing the practice set and submitting your list of missed and unsure items organized by domain. Work that falls short is returned for revision and resubmitted for full credit.
