Match Each Port to Its Protocol

A performance-based question, matching the drag-and-drop format used on the real CompTIA exam.

How This Works

Eight port numbers are shown in the left column, drawn at random from a larger set. For each port, drag the correct protocol onto its slot in the left column, and drag the correct transport type (TCP, UDP, or BOTH) onto its slot in the middle column. Protocol tiles are used once; the TCP/UDP/BOTH tiles can be reused as many times as needed. Click Score to check your answers, and Reset to draw a fresh set of eight ports and shuffle the tiles.
