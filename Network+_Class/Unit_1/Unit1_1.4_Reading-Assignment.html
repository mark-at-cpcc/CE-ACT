How to Read a Certification Text

Read the Exam Essentials first, then the chapter, then the Exam Essentials again. Do the Written Lab as you go. Answer the Review Questions before you look at the key. The reading is where the exam is won; the videos are where it is confirmed.

Terminology Note
None found in Chapters 1–4 against objectives 1.1, 1.5, and 1.6. The book and CompTIA use the same names for the layers, media, connectors, and topologies. One naming note: the book’s “MDF/IDF” section (p. 92) is where objective 2.4’s wiring-closet terms live; you will meet them again in Unit 5.
Beyond the Exam
Chapter 4’s “Ethernet over Other Standards” (pp. 123–126: Ethernet over power line and over HDMI) and Chapter 3’s serial-cable detail (pp. 80–81) exceed the N10-009 objectives. Read them once for context; do not drill them. Everything else in these four chapters is tested.

Reading Notes

Keep one page per chapter. For each: the Exam Essentials rewritten in your own words (if you cannot rewrite one, you do not know it yet); every bolded term with a one-line definition; your answers to the chapter’s Written Lab, checked against Appendix A; and your Review Question score with the numbers you missed. Bring the pages to class — the Wednesday session opens with what the reading left unclear.
