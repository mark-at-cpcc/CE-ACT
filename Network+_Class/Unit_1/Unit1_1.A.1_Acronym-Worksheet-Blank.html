Acronym Research Worksheet

CompTIA Network+ - All Domains

This activity covers 100 acronyms used throughout the CompTIA Network+ (N10-009) exam. For each acronym below, look it up and record two things in your own words: what the acronym stands for, and how it is actually used in networking / IT support. Those are frequently two different answers.

Why This Activity Matters

CompTIA publishes an official acronym list for a reason: acronyms show up constantly on the exam, in scenario questions, and in every job you will do afterward. This worksheet is deliberately old-fashioned — you look each one up yourself, in your own words, rather than being handed the answer. That extra effort is what makes it stick.

How to Research Each One

Search the acronym plus a relevant word — “DHCP networking” works better than “DHCP” alone. Wikipedia, TechTarget, TechTerms, and CompTIA’s own resources are all reliable starting points. Do not copy the first sentence you find; read enough to explain it back in your own words.
