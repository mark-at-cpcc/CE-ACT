No Preparation Needed

Do not study for this and do not attempt it in advance. It measures what you bring with you on day one, which for most of you will be very little — and that is the point. This page stays available all week, and you can retake it as many times as you like once the material starts making sense.

Read This First

You are expected to get questions wrong. Most of you will get most of them wrong, and that is genuinely fine — you have not been taught this yet. The purpose is to prime your brain: research on the testing effect shows that attempting to retrieve an answer you do not have makes the correct answer stick better when you meet it later. Guessing badly today is doing real work.

Select an answer for each question, then click Grade My Answers at the bottom to see your results. Nothing here is locked in until you click that button, and you can click Reset afterward to clear everything and try again.
