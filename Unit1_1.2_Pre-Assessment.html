Read This First

You are expected to get questions wrong. Most of you will get most of them wrong, and that is genuinely fine — you have not been taught this yet. The purpose is to prime your brain: research on the testing effect shows that attempting to retrieve an answer you do not have makes the correct answer stick better when you meet it later. Guessing badly today is doing real work.

📝 Unit 1 Pre-Assessment

Ten questions. Answer first, reveal second.

Question 1

What is the large circuit board that every other component connects to called?

Reveal Answer
The motherboard (also called the system board or mainboard). Everything — processor, memory, storage, expansion cards, power — connects to it either directly or through a cable.
Question 2

Name the three common desktop motherboard form factors, from largest to smallest.

Reveal Answer
ATX, microATX (mATX), and ITX (most commonly Mini-ITX). Form factor determines physical size, mounting hole positions, and how many expansion slots fit.
Question 3

What does RAM stand for, and what happens to its contents when you power off the computer?

Reveal Answer
Random Access Memory. It is volatile — contents are lost the moment power is removed. This is why unsaved work disappears in a power outage.
Question 4

What is the difference between a DIMM and a SO-DIMM?

Reveal Answer
A DIMM (Dual Inline Memory Module) is the full-size memory stick used in desktops and servers. A SO-DIMM (Small Outline DIMM) is the shorter version used in laptops and small-form-factor systems. They are not interchangeable.
Question 5

What does ESD stand for, and why should a technician care about it?

Reveal Answer
Electrostatic Discharge. Static electricity you cannot feel — well under 3,000 volts — can permanently damage a component. Worse, it often causes latent damage that fails weeks later, making the cause nearly impossible to trace.
Question 6

What expansion slot standard is used for modern video cards?

Reveal Answer
PCIe (PCI Express), typically an x16 slot for graphics. PCIe slots come in x1, x4, x8, and x16 widths, indicating the number of data lanes.
Question 7

What is the firmware that runs when you first press the power button, before the operating system loads?

Reveal Answer
The BIOS (Basic Input/Output System), on modern systems implemented as UEFI (Unified Extensible Firmware Interface). It initializes hardware, runs POST, and hands off to the boot device.
Question 8

A computer powers on, but there is no display and it emits a repeating pattern of beeps. What is the system telling you?

Reveal Answer
The POST (Power-On Self-Test) has detected a hardware fault and is reporting it through beep codes, because it cannot yet display text. The pattern maps to a specific fault — frequently memory or video — and the code meaning depends on the firmware manufacturer.
Question 9

Why is thermal paste applied between a CPU and its heat sink?

Reveal Answer
Two metal surfaces that look flat are microscopically rough, trapping insulating air pockets between them. Thermal paste fills those gaps so heat transfers efficiently from the processor into the heat sink.
Question 10

What is the first step in CompTIA's troubleshooting methodology?

Reveal Answer
Identify the problem — gather information, question the user, and determine what changed. Not "start replacing parts." The methodology exists specifically to stop technicians from guessing expensively.
