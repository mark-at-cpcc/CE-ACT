A cumulative check across all five exam domains. Take it before you read anything else in this unit.

Technical Overview

This pre-assessment pulls from every domain on the 220-1201 blueprint rather than from a single unit. Items are weighted toward the domains that carry the most exam questions, with hardware and network troubleshooting appearing most often. You will get a per-domain breakdown when you submit.

In Plain Terms

This one works differently from the pre-assessments in earlier units. Those told you what was coming. This one tells you where to spend your last week. Take it cold — no notes, no textbook, no looking anything up — because a score you propped up is a score that cannot route you anywhere useful. Whatever domain comes back weakest is where your reading, your video time, and your practice questions go for the rest of the course.

How This Counts

This is a diagnostic. Your score is not recorded against your grade and a low result here carries no penalty. Completing it is what counts. Its only job is to give you an honest read on where you stand with the full blueprint in front of you.

Cumulative Pre-Assessment

Select one answer for each item, then submit for your domain breakdown.

What To Do With Your Result

Read the domain breakdown, not just the total. A student at seventy percent overall who missed everything in one domain is in a very different position from a student at seventy percent who missed one item in each. The first has a gap to close and knows exactly where it is. The second needs broad review. Your breakdown tells you which of those two you are.
