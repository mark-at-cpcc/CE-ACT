Test Format

Each attempt draws a fresh random set of 10 questions covering this unit's motherboard, CPU, memory, power, cooling, firmware, and troubleshooting objectives. Click an answer to select it, then click Grade My Answers to score the set. Keep resetting until you score a perfect 10 out of 10.

In Plain Terms

Confident-and-wrong is the category to watch for: you believe something false and have no reason to double-check it. That category is invisible on an ordinary score report, and it is what fails people on the first attempt.

Select an answer for each question, then click Grade My Answers to see your results. Click Reset for a brand-new random set of 10, and keep going. You are expected to repeat this until you score a perfect 10 out of 10 in a single attempt — a fresh random set every time means you cannot simply memorize positions, you actually have to know the material.
