In Plain Terms

Pick one networking concept from this week — a cable type, a piece of hardware, an addressing rule, whatever stuck with you most — and work through it using the four prompts below. Not a summary of the whole week. One concept, examined closely, connected to the real world and to where you're headed professionally.

Full Credit Standard

Full credit requires all four prompts answered directly in the fields below. Responses that summarize rather than reflect, or that address fewer than four prompts, are returned with feedback and resubmitted for full credit. There is no partial score.

Write About What Was Actually Hard

Reflections claiming a concept was easy from the start are the least useful ones to write and the least interesting to read. Nobody is grading you on how quickly you understood something. The struggle and the discovery are where the learning is, and I would far rather read about the ten minutes you spent untangling the 172 private range from the rest of Class B than a paragraph telling me it all made sense right away.
