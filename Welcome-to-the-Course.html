Getting Set Up for Success

Equipment Requirements

To fully participate in our hands-on labs, you will need access to a personal computer. This is a standard requirement for computer professionals, and this course will give you valuable experience with industry-standard tools.

Requirement Details
Device Personal computer required — Chromebooks and tablets are not suitable for this course
Internet Connection Stable, high-speed connection required to access NETLAB and online resources
Minimum Speed 15 Mbps — verify your connection at fast.com
Browser Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge recommended
Please Note

Troubleshooting Wi-Fi connection issues is something the instructor cannot perform remotely. Please make sure your setup meets these requirements before the semester gets underway.

Course Overview

Our class will be managed through two main platforms: Brightspace and NETLAB. Think of Brightspace as your home base — it's where you'll find the course schedule, assignment deadlines, and your grades. Staying organized and keeping track of deadlines is key. Technical issues can happen, so plan ahead and aim to complete assignments before the deadline to avoid last-minute challenges.

Action Needed — Week One

Confirm you can log into NETLAB within the first week of class. If you run into trouble, email me right away so I can help.

How Our Class Works

Mastery Learning Approach

This course uses a "Mastery Learning" approach, which means you are encouraged to work on a topic until you feel confident you've mastered it. While you can practice as much as you like, please be mindful of the weekly due dates for any graded assignments.

Student Expectations

To help you get started smoothly and stay on track, please:

  • Read the course documents in Brightspace — this will answer many common questions about how the class runs and how to submit your work
  • Keep an eye on Brightspace for announcements and reminders about due dates
  • Access your NETLAB account, confirm you can reach the course labs, and complete the assigned work for each chapter using the instructions in our Brightspace shell
  • Check your CPCC email daily — it's the best way to stay informed

Communication

Emailing Your Instructor

When you email me, please use your CPCC student email account and include the full course name and section in the subject line (e.g., 26FA-CTSITS7501-80) — this information is located at the top of the screen in Brightspace. Please use professional language in your communications.

FERPA Requirement

I am not allowed to respond to student emails outside of your CPCC account due to FERPA regulations. Using your CPCC email helps me respond to you quickly and effectively.

Assignment Due Dates

I typically respond to emails within 24 hours during the week. I highly recommend planning your week to avoid waiting until the last minute, as I may not be available to answer questions late on weekends.

Weekly Deadline

All weekly assignments are due on Mondays by 11:59 PM, unless noted otherwise in Brightspace.
