Test Format

Each attempt draws 20 items covering this unit's motherboard, CPU, memory, power, cooling, firmware, and troubleshooting objectives, weighted to mirror how they're represented on the actual 220-1201 exam. Click an answer to select it, then click Grade My Answers to see your results.

In Plain Terms

Because the set is drawn fresh from a large pool, two students — or two attempts by the same student — will rarely see the exact same 20 questions. That's intentional: it rewards actually knowing the material over memorizing a fixed answer key.

Full Credit Standard

Full credit requires completion of all 20 items and a review of any missed questions before moving on — not a specific score. Take the time to understand why an answer was wrong before you close this page.
