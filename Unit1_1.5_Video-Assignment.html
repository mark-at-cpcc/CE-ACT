How to Watch These

Passive watching produces the same warm familiarity as passive reading, and just as little retention. Three habits change that:

  • Pause and predict. When he poses a question or introduces a comparison, pause and answer before he does. Getting it wrong is more useful than hearing it right.
  • One sheet per objective. Not per video — per objective number. All the 3.5 videos land on one page. This mirrors how the exam is organized.
  • Write the summary, not the transcript. After each video, close your notes and write two sentences on what it was about. If you cannot, watch it again at 1.25× speed.

Video and Book Are Not Redundant

Students sometimes ask whether they can skip one. The honest answer is no, and not because I want to be difficult.

Messer is a compression of the objectives — excellent for structure, sequencing, and knowing what matters. The Sybex chapters carry depth, context, and the detail that distinguishes similar answer choices under time pressure. The video tells you a DIMM and a SO-DIMM differ; the book tells you enough that you will still know which is which three weeks from now. Use the video to build the frame and the book to fill it.
