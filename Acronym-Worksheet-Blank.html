Acronym Research Worksheet

100 acronyms from the A+ Core 1 exam. Look up each one and record what it means — and how it's actually used.

This activity covers 100 acronyms used throughout the CompTIA A+ Core 1 (220-1201) exam. For each acronym below, look it up on the internet and record two things in your own words: what the acronym stands for, and how it's actually used in an A+ / IT support context. Those are frequently two different answers — a dictionary definition tells you what the words mean, but it doesn't always tell you why a technician cares.

Why This Activity Matters

CompTIA publishes an official acronym list for a reason: acronyms show up constantly on the exam, in scenario questions, and in every job you'll do afterward. This worksheet is deliberately old-fashioned — you look each one up yourself, in your own words, rather than being handed the answer. That extra effort is what makes it stick.

How to Research Each One

Search the acronym plus a relevant word — "DHCP networking" works better than "DHCP" alone, which can return unrelated results. Wikipedia, TechTarget, and CompTIA's own resources are all reliable starting points. Do not just copy the first sentence you find; read enough to explain it back in your own words.
