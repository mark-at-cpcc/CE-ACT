One Thing That Trips People Up

Take the full-length practice exam seriously and take it timed. Ninety items in ninety minutes, no notes, one sitting. It is the first real read on where you stand, and the number that matters is your scaled score out of 900 — not a percentage. You need 780 or better on two consecutive attempts before I recommend scheduling the real exam. Finding out now where the gaps are is the entire point of doing this in Week 5 rather than Week 6.
