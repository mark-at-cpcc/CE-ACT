Course Overview and Introduction

What this course is designed to accomplish, and what a student who completes it will be able to do.

Course Overview

This course introduces the concepts and issues related to hardware and management of information systems and the networking and security controls of operating systems and endpoints. Upon completion, students should be able to identify hardware types and components as well as recognize software needs for the operating system to manage and control networking between computing devices with the goal of passing the CompTIA A+ Core 1 certification exam.

Course Objectives

Broad, instructor-facing statements of what the course as a whole is designed to accomplish.

  1. Prepare students with no prior computer experience to comprehend, articulate, and apply the foundational hardware, networking, and troubleshooting concepts required by the CompTIA A+ Core 1 (220-1201) certification exam.
  2. Deliver instruction aligned to all five Core 1 exam domains — Mobile Devices, Networking, Hardware, Virtualization and Cloud Computing, and Hardware and Network Troubleshooting — in proportion to their weighting on the official exam.
  3. Build technical competency through direct, hands-on experience with physical computer hardware and live networked systems, rather than through lecture or simulation alone.
  4. Establish a structured, repeatable troubleshooting methodology that students apply consistently across hardware, network, and system-level problems throughout the course.
  5. Instill safe, professional, and legally compliant technical practices, including electrostatic discharge protocols, safe handling of powered components, and appropriate use of diagnostic and lab tools.
  6. Assess readiness for certification through progressively rigorous evaluation, culminating in a scaled practice-exam threshold that predicts a first-attempt pass on the official 220-1201 exam.

Student Learning Outcomes

Specific, measurable statements of what a student who completes the course will be able to do.

Upon successful completion of this course, the student will be able to:

  1. Identify and explain the function of core computer hardware components — including motherboards, processors, memory, storage devices, power supplies, and cooling systems — by physical inspection and written assessment.
  2. Configure and troubleshoot fundamental networking components and services, including IP addressing, common network protocols and ports, wireless connectivity, and SOHO network configuration, using both live lab environments and written scenarios.
  3. Apply a structured, six-step troubleshooting methodology to diagnose and resolve hardware, network, and mobile device problems presented as realistic technical scenarios.
  4. Describe virtualization and cloud computing concepts, including hypervisor types, deployment models, and service models, and explain their practical application in a business environment.
  5. Demonstrate exam readiness for the CompTIA A+ Core 1 (220-1201) certification by achieving a scaled score of 780 or higher on two consecutive full-length practice examinations.
